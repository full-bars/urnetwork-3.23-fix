### 🚀 High-Scale Performance Tuning
This release optimizes the provider for environments with 4,000+ proxies and high signaling load.

**Changes:**
*   **Initial Contract Size**: Increased from 256KB to **2MB** to reduce signaling 'spam' during connection startup.
*   **Signaling Timeout**: Increased from 30s to **60s** to handle signaling backlogs without exiting.
*   **Contract Fill Fraction**: Lowered from 0.8 to **0.7** to provide a larger safety buffer for contract refills.
*   **Documentation**: Updated README to reflect high-scale stability improvements.
