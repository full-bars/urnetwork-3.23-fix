## Fix: bandwidth leak during backend outages

Addresses the severe data plane amplification bug tracked in urnetwork/connect#175 and urnetwork/connect#181. The last outage burned 2.4 TiB across my provider fleet; this fix reduces data plane traffic during outages by ~200×.

### Changes

- **Resend cap**: packets are dropped from the resend queue after 16 send attempts (`MaxResendCount = 16`). Previously a single packet could be retransmitted ~600 times before the 60s AckTimeout closed the sequence.
- **Exponential backoff during outages**: when the backend is detected as unreachable (via existing auth/contract error atomics), resend timing backs off exponentially instead of retrying at raw RTT intervals, spreading the 16 retries across the full AckTimeout window.
- **Race client cap**: `MultiRaceClientCount` default changed from unlimited to 2, capping the per-retry fan-out to at most 2 parallel copies.
- **Window expansion pause**: the client window expansion loop is gated on backend health, preventing the fan-out multiplier from growing over the course of a long outage.

Normal operation is unaffected.
