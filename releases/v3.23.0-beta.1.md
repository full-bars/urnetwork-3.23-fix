### 🧪 Beta: Automated Proxy Recovery & Smart Backoff

This pre-release implements a fully automated background retry mechanism for offline proxies, designed to recover from network instability without disturbing healthy connections.

#### 🚀 Key Features:
- **Global Reset Pulse**: Every **60 minutes**, the provider triggers a global pulse that wakes up all sleeping/dead proxies and resets their health counters. This gives them a fresh chance to connect without needing to restart the service.
- **Smart Exponential Backoff**: Proxies that fail repeatedly will now wait longer between attempts (5s, 10s, 20s... up to 1 hour). This significantly reduces CPU usage and log noise on servers with many offline proxies.
- **Warmup Protection**: The pulse signal is only processed by **disconnected** proxies. Active, healthy connections ignore the signal entirely, ensuring their 8-hour warmup and throughput stability are never interrupted.
